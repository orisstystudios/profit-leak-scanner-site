What it checks

The leaks most stores never see on a dashboard

Your sales report shows what came in. It does not show the orders you lost to a missing return promise, or the margin a blanket discount gave away. That is what Auditink looks for.

Margin Pricing and discounts

  • Discounts deeper than your margin allows. Average discount measured against gross margin.
  • Heavy compare-at markdowns. Products marked down 35% or more.
  • Free shipping threshold too low. Set so close to your average order that it rarely lifts basket size.
  • Ad cost squeezing profit. What you pay per customer against profit per order.

Returns Refunds and fit

  • High return rate. Flagged once refunds pass the level where most operators start digging.
  • Return signals in reviews. Phrases like "runs small" or "not as pictured".
  • Missing size or fit guidance. Apparel and shoes with no size chart or fit notes.

Trust Reasons shoppers hesitate

  • Return promise hidden. Only on a policy page, not near the buy button.
  • No shipping timeline on the product page.
  • Weak social proof. No reviews, ratings or testimonials where people decide.
  • Support hard to find before purchase.

Catalog Product pages

  • Thin descriptions. Products with fewer than 20 words of copy.
  • Missing product images.
  • No product FAQ answering shipping, sizing and material questions.
  • Contradicting claims about materials, care or delivery speed.

Honest by design

No scare numbers. When the data can't support a figure, we say so.

Plenty of tools will tell you that you are "losing $50,000 a month". When it can't, the estimate is withheld and the catalog findings still stand.

Questions

Before you install

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Auditink only asks for read access. It never creates, edits or deletes anything in your store, and it stores no customer personal data. See the privacy policy for the details.

What do I get on the free plan?

A full audit of your store: a risk verdict, the complete list of findings with the top fixes, and a CSV export of catalog issues. Money estimates, scheduled rescans and history are part of Growth Monitor.

Why would the report withhold a money estimate?

Because guessing would be worse than saying nothing. If your products have no cost prices, or there are too few orders to measure, Auditink tells you so. Adding product costs in Shopify usually unlocks the estimates.

Do I need to set anything up?

No. Install it and press Scan. The audit uses the products, policies and orders already in your store.

How do I cancel Growth Monitor?

Downgrade from the plan section inside the app, or uninstall it from your Shopify admin. Billing runs through Shopify, so there is nothing else to cancel.
